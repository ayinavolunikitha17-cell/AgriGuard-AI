# 🌾 AgriGuard AI

### AI-Powered Crop Disease Detection & Climate-Aware Farming Advisory

AgriGuard AI is an AI-powered agricultural web application designed to help farmers identify crop diseases and receive practical, weather-aware treatment recommendations.

The application acts as a real-time bridge between **field conditions and expert agronomic guidance** by combining crop images, farmer location, disease analysis, and live weather information.

---

## 🚜 Problem Statement

Farmers often face difficulties in identifying crop diseases at an early stage and deciding the correct treatment. Unpredictable weather conditions can make the situation more challenging because spraying pesticides or applying treatments at the wrong time can reduce effectiveness and increase crop losses.

Traditional agricultural advice may also require farmers to consult experts or visit agricultural centers, which can take valuable time.

Therefore, there is a need for an intelligent system that can:

- Identify crop diseases from images.
- Understand the farmer's location.
- Analyze current and upcoming weather conditions.
- Provide clear treatment recommendations.
- Suggest suitable and safer times for agricultural activities.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

AgriGuard AI provides farmers with a simple web interface where they can submit:

📷 **Crop/Leaf Image**  
🌦️ **Weather Information**

The system analyzes these inputs and generates an actionable agricultural advisory.
